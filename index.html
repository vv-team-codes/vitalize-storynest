Every child deserves a story made just for them.

Create picture books, animations, songs and comics in your browser. Stories, characters and illustrations are generated here — no account, no payment, no upload to a server. Your work stays in this browser (localStorage).

Built-in engine only. External AI providers (Runway / Pika / Luma / Kling / Veo, LLM, image, music) are optional in Settings — a key shows “Key saved — not verified” until a live test passes. Nothing here fakes a video or a melody.

How it works

1 · Idea

Type an idea, paste a manuscript, upload a .txt file, or pick a suggested idea.

2 · Story & Characters

Pick age, type, language and pages. The engine writes the title, moral, characters and page-by-page text. The Character Bible locks face, hair, outfit and colours so every picture matches.

3 · Illustrate & Export

Generate a picture for every page in 10 styles, edit in the book editor, read aloud, and export a watermark-free PDF.

Built-in video renderer (no key needed)

Renders your storyboard scenes on a canvas with slow zoom / gentle pan, captions and narration text, and records it with MediaRecorder. The file downloads from this browser. This is a Ken-Burns-style render of your generated illustrations — it is not an external AI video model.

Tip: click Render once — the preview below plays live while it records. A 15s test is fastest; 1 min takes a full minute. Keep this tab open until it says Done.
